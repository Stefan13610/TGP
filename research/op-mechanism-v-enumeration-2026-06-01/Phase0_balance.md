  LIGO scalar amplitude without a genuinely new ingredient (R1 flag).

3. Flag any PARTIAL_OVERLAP (e.g., candidate (b) ⊂ R1 #20 scope)

**Binary-structural justification (LOCKED):** "VIABLE" = there exists a self-consistent structural pathway,
grounded in LOCKED results, by which the candidate COULD (in a future dedicated cycle) address the LIGO scalar
amplitude WITHOUT violating any LOCKED verdict or introducing a new fundamental constant. "NOT_VIABLE" = the
candidate is structurally blocked by a LOCKED result. This is an assessment of pathway existence, NOT a claim of
realization.
